Planned versus spent

In its first three years the fund took in $233 million more than budgeted and spent $67 million less, the County Auditor found, and the balance has grown each year since. The most recent year with a published breakdown of actual spending is FY2024, from the County Auditor.

The county pays providers $18,228 per school-year seat and $26,004 per full-year seat in FY2027, for every contracted seat whether or not it is filled. The line item also covers start-up, transportation and inclusion funds and the county staff who manage contracts, so it runs higher than the seat rate.

What the public record stops short of

  • Payments to individual preschools. The county publishes the list of sites but no dollar amount per provider. Contract amounts exist in each provider contract and in the county's accounting system; a public records request to Multnomah County is the way to get them.
  • Subcontracts. Facilities grants flow through BuildUp Oregon and small-provider payments flow through intermediary organizations. The county budget shows the total sent to them, and the auditor's report lists facilities awards by type for FY2024.
  • Actual spending after FY2024 by category. The county's annual financial report gives fund totals. Category-level actuals have only been published by the Auditor.
